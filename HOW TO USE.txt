Premium Health — Employee Appreciation Certificates
===================================================


STEP 1 — Open the certificate builder
--------------------------------------
Mac:     Double-click  "Open Digital Certificate — Mac.command"
Windows: Double-click  "Open Digital Certificate — Windows.bat"

Your browser opens automatically with the certificate. A "Tweaks" panel
appears in the bottom-right corner where you fill in the employee's name,
role, award title, citation, and more.


STEP 2 — Customize the certificate
------------------------------------
In the Tweaks panel (bottom-right), edit:
  • Name, role, and gender
  • Award title and certificate number
  • Date and citation text
  • Signer names and titles
  • Background color, shine, and motion effects


STEP 3 — Save or print
-----------------------
  Digital (PNG image):  Click "Download PNG" (top-right corner of the screen)
  Printable PDF:        Open  "app / Print Certificate.html"
                        then use File > Print > Save as PDF in your browser


---

ALTERNATIVE — Open without the launcher
-----------------------------------------
If you prefer to just double-click an HTML file directly, go into the
"app" folder and open:

  "Digital Certificate.html"  — interactive version with Tweaks panel
  "Print Certificate.html"    — clean printable layout (letter landscape)

Both files work by double-clicking — no launcher needed.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Tweaks panel not showing?
  → Look for a small "Tweaks" button in the bottom-right and click it.
  → Or use the launcher (Step 1 above) instead of double-clicking the HTML.

Browser says "can't open file"?
  → Use the launcher (Step 1) — it starts a local server for you.

Fonts look wrong?
  → The "app/fonts" folder must stay next to the HTML files. Do not move
    files out of the "app" folder individually.
